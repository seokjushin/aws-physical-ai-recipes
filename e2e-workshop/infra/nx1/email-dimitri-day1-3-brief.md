I dry-ran the full Day 1 build end-to-end on NX1 and it now completes cleanly in ~30 min. Two environment-specific notes for your team (FYI, both handled on our side):
- **IPv6 egress from the private subnets appears to black-hole** (the shared NAT looks IPv4-only). Dual-stack instances try AAAA first and stall on connection timeouts before falling back to IPv4, which roughly doubled build time until we forced IPv4 on the instance. You may want to confirm whether IPv6 egress is intended for `SubnetApp*`.
- **Session Manager enforces KMS-encrypted sessions** (`alias/ssm/logging`); we added `kms:Decrypt` on that key to our instance role so sessions establish.
